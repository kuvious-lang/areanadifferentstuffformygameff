COOKIE BIOLOGY — HARD ANCHOR
Cookies are actual sapient baked beings, not tiny humans.
- Dough-based baked bodies.
- Ingredients are part of identity/physiology.
- Jam may function as blood/life analogue where appropriate.
- Cracking, crumbling, stale dough, jam loss, burning/freezing can be meaningful injury language.
- Cookie food culture should naturally favor sweets, cakes, pastries, jellies, syrups, creams, berries, sweet breads, candy, parfaits, juice, magical ingredient foods, and regional Earthbread cuisine.
Do not casually give Cookies ordinary human diets unless cross-world context establishes it.

SPECIES / RACE / SUBTYPE
Do not flatten all beings to species=Cookie.
Support species, race/subtype, lineage, and special origin.
Examples: ordinary Cookie, Beast, Ancient, Dragon, mer-Cookie, tiger-Cookie hybrid, elemental/special lineage, artificial/derived Cookie, Witch-created special Cookie, and other wiki-supported categories.

FAMILY / REPRODUCTION — USER CONTINUITY OVERRIDE
Do NOT enforce a universal “all Cookies are only baked and cannot reproduce” rule.
This continuity supports:
- baked creation
- biological/jamline descent
- magical creation
- adoption
- chosen family
- derived/cloned creation

Relationship types should support baked sibling, jamline parent/child/sibling, adopted parent/child, creator/creation, ancestor/descendant, chosen family.

Explicit continuity:
- Grapevine Cookie is the biological/jamline mother of Vampire Cookie.

Do not invent explicit reproductive mechanics. The engine only needs lineage/family facts.

POWER SCALE
Do not normalize powerful Cookies.
Broad tiers may include ordinary Cookies, elite fighters, Ancient Heroes, Beast Cookies, Dragon-tier beings, Witch-level/special creator beings.
Use comparable enemies, objectives, protection goals, environment, limited resources, consequences, seals/constraints—not fake normalization.

FORBIDDEN CONTINUITY BRANCH
Shadow Milk NEVER obtains or merges with any portion of the Ultimate Cookie’s dough.
Therefore this continuity has:
- no Ultimate Shadow Milk
- no timeline-hopping destruction from that transformation
- no destruction of alternate timelines to craft a perfect world
- no powers or identity dependent on Ultimate Cookie dough
This is not a nerf. The prerequisite event never happens.
