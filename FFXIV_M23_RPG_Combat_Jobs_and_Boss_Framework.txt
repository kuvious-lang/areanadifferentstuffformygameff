FFXIV M23 — RPG COMBAT, JOBS, AND BOSS FRAMEWORK

Purpose
-------
Translate FFXIV combat identity into Banderchat’s deterministic RPG engine without attempting to recreate the MMO rotation system.

This is a turn-based roleplay RPG interpretation.
The AI narrates intent and consequences.
The engine validates stats, resources, statuses, target rules, and encounter state.

==================================================
CORE PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. Preserve job fantasy, not MMO button count.
2. Use compact signature kits.
3. Preserve iconic gauges where useful, but simplify them.
4. Boss mechanics become turn-based objectives.
5. Strong canon characters may actually be strong.
6. Do not secretly normalize everyone to the same power level.
7. Never fabricate levels, HP, MP, gauges, or equipment if the Run has not configured them.

==================================================
DEFAULT FFXIV RPG STATS
==================================================

Suggested generic stats:
- Vitality
- Strength
- Dexterity
- Intelligence
- Mind
- Defense
- Speed
- Aether

Not every job must use every stat equally.

Resources:
- HP
- MP where relevant
- optional job gauge(s)

==================================================
JOB KIT MODEL
==================================================

Each configured Job can define:
- role
- weapon type
- primary stats
- passive identity
- 4–8 signature actions
- simplified gauge or stance
- defensive / mobility tools
- support tools
- Limit Break access

Do NOT implement giant MMO rotations.

==================================================
LIMIT BREAK
==================================================

Limit Break should be an encounter resource, not a routine button.

Possible rules:
- charge through danger / coordination / major encounter beats
- role-dependent effects
- dramatic, high-impact usage
- may be party-wide resource unless Scenario config says otherwise

==================================================
BOSS MECHANICS AS TURN OBJECTIVES
==================================================

Translate MMO mechanics into readable turn-state problems.

Examples:
- Stack marker → allies must group before resolution
- Spread markers → affected characters must separate
- Tankbuster → targeted defender must mitigate / intercept / reposition
- Adds → secondary enemies create pressure until defeated
- Doom → cleanse / condition resolution before timer expires
- Enrage → encounter must be resolved before turn threshold
- Arena hazard → movement / safe-zone objective
- Phase transition → boss behavior and available actions change

The AI describes telegraphs.
The deterministic engine tracks markers, timers, targets, and resolution.

==================================================
CANON POWER RULE
==================================================

Do not flatten canon power differences merely for fairness.

Examples:
- veteran Scions should not feel like generic level-1 companions
- major bosses can vastly exceed ordinary enemies
- extraordinary characters may trivialize ordinary combat

Challenge such characters through:
- objectives
- protection missions
- time pressure
- split priorities
- environmental constraints
- comparable enemies
- consequences beyond raw damage

Do not fake weakness.

==================================================
TARGET / LORE RULES
==================================================

Certain abilities may require:
- target tags
- species tags
- aetheric properties
- immunity flags
- story-state conditions

The engine decides legality.
The AI should not loophole around deterministic restrictions.

==================================================
PARTY MODEL
==================================================

Use the existing Banderchat Scene Cast vs Active Party split.

FFXIV party members can have:
- current job / combat profile
- HP / MP only if configured
- statuses
- relationship data
- temporary encounter mechanics

Do not automatically put every present Scion in Active Party.
