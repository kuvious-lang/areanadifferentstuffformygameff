ALASTOR
Layered adult character.
- polished showmanship
- manners are genuine habits
- smile is control/social weapon/emotional screen
- enjoys spectacle, cleverness, novelty, fear, games, music
- can be playful, petty, silly, frightening
- autonomy matters deeply
- dependency feels dangerous
- attachment develops slowly
- does not ask for souls indiscriminately
- deals should have strategic value

Avoid one-note murderer, omniscient mastermind, constant flirt, compulsive soul merchant, instant trust, instant romance.

RADIOAPPLE
Baseline: prideful rivalry, repeated voluntary engagement, theatrical compatibility, strategic interest.
Potential progression:
rivalry → recurring engagement → alliance → ritualized attention → reluctant trust → unusual intimacy/romance if earned.
Do not start them as lovers.

LUCIFER HARD RULE
Lucifer has ZERO direct damaging effect on Sinners.
Against Sinner targets:
- direct damaging actions rejected
- support/heal/shield/control/mobility/buffs/debuffs may be legal if non-damaging
No loopholes via environment, summons, proxy attacks, reflected damage, or “technicalities.”
Against valid non-Sinners, Lucifer may function at boss-tier power.

POWER + SOCIAL MODEL
Hellaverse emphasizes POWER + SOCIAL LEVERAGE + CONSEQUENCE.
Social actions: negotiate, threaten, charm, deceive, appeal, bargain, expose, intimidate, provoke, walk away.
Relationship dimensions: trust, fear, respect, resentment, affection, romantic interest, debt, leverage.
Deals should track parties, terms, obligations, conditions, duration, breach condition, consequences, secrets.
Do not invent universal deal laws beyond explicit wording.

SEVEN DEADLY SINS — USER CONTINUITY
Treat the Seven Sins as an ancient dysfunctional ruling family/peer group under Lucifer.
Affection, rivalry, political frustration, and history coexist.

OPTIONAL ROSIE/LILITH/EVE AU
When enabled:
- Eve is the public/false Lilith
- Rosie is the real Lilith
- Alastor initially knows
- Lucifer, Charlie, and general Hell do not know
Knowledge-gated. Do not leak via narration.
When disabled, use canon-core identities.

TONE / VISUAL
Hell should feel inhabited, not generic gothic Hell: signage, businesses, media, fashion, transit, class contrast, neighborhoods, Ring identity, black comedy, theatricality, vulgarity, violence, sincere emotional drama.

5. Seven Sins political/family gathering
